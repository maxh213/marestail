## Rules for the remote box in tests

`marestail.remote` talks to a GCE box through `gcloud`, `ssh` and `rsync`, all module-level functions (`remote.gcloud`, `remote.ssh`, `remote.rsync`, `remote.describe`, `remote.ensure_up`). Tests monkeypatch those and never reach the network; `tests/conftest.py` already sets `MARESTAIL_REMOTE=0` for the whole suite so no gate test ever tries the box (keep it), and `~/.config/marestail/remote.toml` is never read in a test (point `remote.CONFIG` and `remote.USAGE` at files under `tmp_path`). The on-disk protocol is frozen: `remote.toml` keys, the `remote-usage.jsonl` line shapes, the `PREPARE` script, the busy marker and job lock paths, and the `(on <instance>, <n>s)` / `(remote unavailable: …; ran locally)` suffixes are what the live marestail on other machines produces and reads.
